My Life as an IT Student

More Than Just Coding

IT Journey

Being an IT student is not just about computers and codes; it is also about pressure, patience, and perseverance. Many times napapatanong ako kung tama ba ang pinili kong course, especially kapag sunod sunod ang deadlines at parang hindi ko agad maintindihan ang lessons. Pero kahit ganun, I still continue because deep inside I know this path will help me grow. This blog is about my personal experience as an IT student, my struggles, realizations, and small victories along the way. Hindi madali ang pagiging IT student. May mga subjects na sobrang hirap intindihin, lalo na kapag programming na at puro logic ang kailangan. Minsan kahit ilang oras na akong nagco code, ayaw pa rin gumana ang program dahil lang sa isang maliit na error. Nakaka stress at nakaka frustrate, pero tinutulak ko pa rin ang sarili ko na tapusin ang ginagawa ko at hanapin ang solution. Sa bawat bug na naaayos ko, may bagong lesson akong natutunan, hindi lang tungkol sa code kundi tungkol din sa tiyaga at problem solving. Unti unti kong naiintindihan na ang pagiging IT student ay training hindi lang para maging magaling sa technical skills kundi para maging matatag, resourceful, at handa sa mga real world challenges na haharapin ko sa future.

Essential Tools

As an IT student, I honestly cannot imagine my daily tasks without tools like Canva, Google Workspace, and virtual machines. Canva helps me express ideas visually kahit wala akong strong design background, allowing me to create presentations, infographics, and project materials that look clean and professional. Meanwhile, tools like Google Docs and Google Drive under Google Workspace keep my files organized and accessible kahit saan ako mag login, which is very helpful during group projects and deadline heavy weeks. I can collaborate in real time, share files instantly, and never worry too much about losing my work. Virtual machines naman give me the freedom to experiment without fear of breaking my main system. I can install different operating systems, test configurations, and explore system settings safely, which really helped me become more confident in handling technical tasks. These tools do not just make work easier they make learning more hands on, practical, and less stressful. Because of them, I feel more prepared, efficient, and capable as I continue my journey in the IT field.

The AI Impact

Napapansin ko na sobrang laki na ng impact ng AI sa IT field ngayon, lalo na sa way we learn and work. From intelligent code suggestions to faster debugging, AI tools help me understand concepts na dati sobrang hirap intindihin at minsan inaabot pa ng ilang oras bago ko ma grasp. With platforms like ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot, mas napapabilis ang workflow ko because they can explain errors, suggest optimizations, and provide examples that guide me step by step, parang mentor na available anytime especially during late night coding sessions. At the same time, may konting pressure din kasi mas mataas na ang expectations sa IT students and professionals ngayon since AI can generate code quickly, kaya kailangan mas maging critical thinker at hindi puwedeng copy paste lang without understanding the logic behind it. For me, AI feels like a powerful assistant, not a replacement, but only if marunong kang gumamit at mag isip pa rin on your own, at naniniwala ako na ang tunay na advantage ay nasa tao pa rin na marunong mag analyze, mag create, at mag innovate beyond what AI can suggest.
